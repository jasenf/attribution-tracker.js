Choose the right starting point

HTML forms and Webflow cover the field handoff. WordPress explains script installation and the boundary with form plugins. Jekyll covers shared layouts and static form endpoints. Squarespace and Wix cover browser capture and explain why their native forms need a separate adapter.

The tracker stores first-touch data in the browser after consent. It does not submit forms or connect to a CRM on its own. Each platform guide includes checks for your published site.
